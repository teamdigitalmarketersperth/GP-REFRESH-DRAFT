Speaker expression of interest · 2027

Teach what GPs need to know, in 6 minutes.

GP Refresh invites specialists and specialist GPs to distil a 1-hour lecture into one focused 6-minute TED-style talk, followed by a 30-minute panel discussion.

  • Melbourne · Sydney · Brisbane
  • 6-minute talks
  • 30-min panels
Express interest

The format

One topic. One learning outcome. Six minutes.

Every talk covers the essentials of a common GP presentation: what to look for, what to do, and what has changed. Delegates send questions through the GP Refresh app, and the panel answers them after each block of talks.

  1. 6-minute TED-style talkPractical, evidence-based and sponsor-free.
  2. 30-minute panel discussionAnswer delegate questions with fellow speakers.
  3. Reviewed by the Scientific CommitteeChaired by Dr Justin Coleman, Editor of Murtagh's General Practice.
