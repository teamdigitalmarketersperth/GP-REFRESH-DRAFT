JOIN THE 6-MINUTE
CPD REVOLUTION

GP Refresh delivers 6-minute presentations across 77 clinical topics spanning the length and breadth of General Practice, followed by Q&A over two conference days in one weekend. Guided by a Scientific Committee chaired by Dr Justin Coleman, Editor of the current edition of John Murtagh’s General Practice, and a team of experienced GPs, the program draws on decades of combined clinical, medical education and professional development experience. It delivers 77+ evidence-based learning outcomes designed to be concise, relevant, practical and directly applicable in practice the very next day.

GP Refresh keeps the educational agenda focused on what matters most to GPs, with no sponsored talks or commercial influence shaping the program.
