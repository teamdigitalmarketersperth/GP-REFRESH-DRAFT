Programs we offer

Our Future CPD Training Programs

We offer a range of CPD training options tailored to meet the diverse needs of General Practitioners across Australia:

01
Online Learning Modules
  • Flexible, self-paced online courses covering various topics such as chronic disease management, mental health, and preventive care.
  • Interactive quizzes and assessments to reinforce learning.
02
Workshops and Seminars
  • In-person and virtual workshops led by experienced facilitators and industry experts.
  • Opportunities for hands-on practice, case studies, and group discussions.
03
Webinars
  • Live and recorded sessions on current topics in general practice, allowing you to learn from the comfort of your own home or office.
  • Access to expert speakers and Q&A sessions for deeper understanding.
04
Clinical Skills Training
  • Practical training sessions focused on enhancing clinical skills, including procedures, diagnostics, and patient communication.
  • Simulation-based learning to build confidence and competence.
05
Mentorship Programs
  • Pairing with experienced GPs for one-on-one mentorship to discuss clinical cases, practice management, and career development.
  • Networking opportunities with peers in the field.
