## FAQs
## Find the answers to all commonly asked questions
## I’m not a GP - can I still attend and claim CPD hours? 
 Yes. GP Refresh Welcomes any Medical Practitioner Including Non-GP Specialists and Hospital Doctors/Registered Nurse/Allied Health Professionals with an AHPRA number as the content is relevant to everyone.
## How many CPD hours can I earn? 
 You can earn up to 53 CPD hours by completing the pre- and post-conference activities and attending the full conference. RACGP CPD Home hours are allocated directly by GP Refresh and for rest all CPD homes, we will provide the confirmation for you to upload yourself.
## Does this count towards CPD requirements? 
 Yes. The conference is accredited by relevant national colleges. Certificates are provided to support appraisal and revalidation.
To view our accreditors, please click here
## Is the content relevant to everyday general practice? 
 Yes. Sessions are practical, case-based, and focused on common presentations, updated guidelines, and prescribing changes you can apply immediately. We believe that there will be a practice changing learning point for every 53+ clinical topics.
## Can I tailor the programme to my interests? 
 No, however the programme covers the entire length and breadth of General Practice.
## Are sessions interactive? 
 Yes. Throughout the 60 mins of presentations, delegates have the opportunity to asking questions through  the GP Refresh App , which will be addressed in the next 30 minutes of panel discussion.This format provides an interactive and informative experience, facilitating the sharing of clinical knowledge.
## Will session recordings be available? 
 Yes. Recordings will be available to registered delegates for 4 weeks after the conference.
## Can I attend online? 
 Yes. GP Refresh offers online attendance options.
## Is there a CPR Refresher session? 
 Yes. The CPR Refresher is complimentary for all attendees.
## Are accommodation discounts available? 
 Yes. Accommodation packages and discounted rates are available.Accommodation options coming soon.
## Will there be opportunities to network? 
 Yes. Dedicated networking time is built into the programme so you can connect with peers and share experiences during Breakfast, Tea and Lunch breaks along with the Gala Dinner and Welcome Reception.
## When and where are future conferences held? 
 GP Refresh is held annually across major Australian cities, including Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane and Internationally in Dubai and International Cruises. Dates and locations vary each year.
Please visit the website for the latest updates.
## What sponsorship packages are available? 
 A range of sponsorship and exhibition packages are available, including exhibition booths, branding placements, and digital exposure. A sponsorship prospectus outlining inclusions and pricing is available on request.
## What is included in an exhibition booth package? 
 Standard inclusions typically cover exhibition space, exhibitor passes*, branding opportunities, and catering for booth representatives. Specific inclusions vary depending on the package level.
## How can sponsors engage with delegates? 
 Sponsors can connect with delegates during dedicated exhibition time, refreshment breaks held in the exhibition area, and networking sessions designed to maximise interaction.
## How many delegates are expected? 
 Attendance numbers vary by city, however GP Refresh expects 1000+ delegates at each conference in Australia.
## Can sponsors participate in multiple cities? 
 Yes. Multi-city packages are available for organisations wishing to exhibit across the full conference series.
## What marketing exposure is included? 
 Depending on the package, sponsors may receive logo placement on the conference website, event materials, email marketing, social media promotion, and on-site signage.
## When will logistical details be provided? 
 An Exhibitor Manual will be distributed prior to the event, outlining bump-in and bump-out times, venue access, freight information, and on-site requirements.
## Are there compliance requirements? 
 Yes. All sponsorship activities must comply with relevant industry regulations. Sponsors are responsible for ensuring their materials meet applicable compliance standards.
## How do we secure our sponsorship? 
 Sponsorship is confirmed upon receipt of a signed agreement and payment. Spaces are limited and allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.
