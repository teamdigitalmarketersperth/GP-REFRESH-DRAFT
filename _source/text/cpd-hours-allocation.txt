## Pre-Conference Activity
 All registered delegates will receive access to pre-conference learning materials designed to prepare and align learning with RACGP standards.

## Activity Overview 
 Includes selected chapters from Murtagh’s General Practice (latest edition), provided through the RACGP website.
 Ensures alignment with college-endorsed resources and CPD requirements.
 Learning begins before the conference itself, maximising the value of live sessions.
## Breakdown 
 Educational Activities (16 hours): Guided reading across key clinical chapters to build a foundation for conference learning.
 Reviewing Performance (3 hours): Clinical scenario questions and reflective exercises to test knowledge, identify learning gaps, and benchmark against best-practice guidelines.
 Note: Completion of this pre-conference activity supports RACGP’s CPD requirements and prepares delegates for in-person
sessions.
