## Who We Are
 At GP Refresh, we are a team of healthcare professionals passionate about providing high-impact, practical education for GPs and primary care clinicians. With decades of combined experience in clinical practice, medical education, and professional development, we understand the challenges of modern general practice and are dedicated to making learning concise, relevant, and directly applicable.
Our programs are guided by our Advisory Committee and Scientific Committee, made up of experienced clinicians, specialists, and educators. Their expertise ensures every session is evidence-based, clinically rigorous, and focused on improving patient care. We believe education should fit the realities of practice, which is why our conferences, hybrid programs, and digital resources deliver time-efficient learning without compromising quality.
Through GP Refresh, we bring together the best minds in primary care to share practical insights, emerging treatments, and current guidelines. We welcome all healthcare professionals involved in primary care GPs, GP Registrars, and other clinicians to join us in enhancing knowledge, refining skills, and ultimately improving outcomes for patients.
At the heart of GP Refresh is a small, dedicated team working behind the scenes to ensure every event is seamless and impactful. Our Business Manager oversees operations and strategic planning, while the Events Manager ensures each conference runs smoothly. Our CPD & Partnerships Manager ensures all content meets RACGP standards and cultivates strategic partnerships with healthcare organisations, education providers, and industry collaborators to support high-quality learning for clinicians. Together, this passionate and experienced team brings attention to detail, expertise, and energy, making every GP Refresh event practical, relevant, and engaging for all participants.

## Who is GP Refresh?
## Empowering General Practitioners with Knowledge
 GP Refresh is run by a Group which has 30 years of experience in HealthCare industries across the World including UK. GP Refresh is an Innovative 2-day Conference, where, about 100 Clinical Topics across the Full length and breadth of General Practice are covered. Each clinical Topic is allocated up to 10 mins with initial talk focusing on Basics of GP consult with pathognomonic clinical features (in other words “GP Refresh”) followed by latest clinical evidence based updates.
The GP Refresh welcomes all healthcare professionals working in or with an interest in primary care, including GPs & GP Registrars.

## Our Purpose
## to Serve as an Expert
 In a field as dynamic as healthcare, staying updated is essential. GP Refresh was created to serve as a trusted source for the latest medical insights, Continuing Professional Development (CPD) resources, and training modules tailored specifically to GPs. We aim to make it easy for you to keep up with advancements, fulfill CPD requirements, and refine your skills to provide the highest standard of care.
## Programs we offer
## Our Future CPD Training Programs
 We offer a range of CPD training options tailored to meet the diverse needs of General Practitioners across Australia:
 01 
## Online Learning Modules
 Flexible, self-paced online courses covering various topics such as chronic disease management, mental health, and preventive care.
 Interactive quizzes and assessments to reinforce learning.
 02 
## Workshops and Seminars
 In-person and virtual workshops led by experienced facilitators and industry experts.
 Opportunities for hands-on practice, case studies, and group discussions.
 03 
## Webinars
 Live and recorded sessions on current topics in general practice, allowing you to learn from the comfort of your own home or office.
 Access to expert speakers and Q&A sessions for deeper understanding.
 04 
## Clinical Skills Training
 Practical training sessions focused on enhancing clinical skills, including procedures, diagnostics, and patient communication.
 Simulation-based learning to build confidence and competence.
 05 
## Mentorship Programs
 Pairing with experienced GPs for one-on-one mentorship to discuss clinical cases, practice management, and career development.
 Networking opportunities with peers in the field.
