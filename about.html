GP Refresh · The Global Family Doctors Congress

Practical education that fits the realities of practice.

GP Refresh brings the best minds in primary care together to share practical insights, emerging treatments and current guidelines, in 6-minute talks GPs can apply the next day.

Who we are

Built by clinicians, for clinicians.

GP Refresh is run by healthcare professionals with decades of combined experience in clinical practice, medical education and professional development. We understand the pressures of modern general practice, so we keep learning concise, relevant and directly applicable.

Our program is guided by a Scientific Committee and an Advisory Committee of experienced clinicians, specialists and educators. Their oversight keeps every session evidence-based, clinically rigorous and independent.

We welcome everyone working in primary care, including GPs, GP registrars, non-GP specialists, hospital doctors, nurses and allied health professionals with an AHPRA number.
