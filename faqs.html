Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

Can't find your answer? Call 1300 GP REFRESH (1300 477 337) or email connect@gprefresh.com.

For delegates

I'm not a GP. Can I still attend and claim CPD hours?

Yes. GP Refresh welcomes any medical practitioner, including non-GP specialists and hospital doctors, as well as registered nurses and allied health professionals with an AHPRA number.

How many CPD hours can I earn?

Up to 53 CPD hours, by attending the full Congress and completing the pre- and post-Congress activities. RACGP CPD Home hours are allocated directly by GP Refresh. For other CPD homes, we provide confirmation for you to upload.

Is GP Refresh accredited?

Yes. GP Refresh is an accredited CPD provider with RACGP and ACRRM, and RNZCGP members can claim up to 80 CME credits. Certificates are provided.

What is a 6-minute talk?

Leading clinicians distil a 1-hour lecture into a 6-minute TED-style talk with one clear learning outcome. There are 70+ clinical topics across two days.

Are sessions interactive?

Yes. During each block of talks, delegates send questions through the GP Refresh app. The questions are answered in the 30-minute panel discussion that follows.

Are there sponsored talks?

No. Every talk is independent and evidence-based, with no commercial influence over what you learn.

Will session recordings be available?

Yes. Recordings are available to registered delegates for 4 weeks after the Congress.

Can I attend online?

Yes. GP Refresh offers online attendance options.

Is the Gala Dinner included?

Yes. The Grand Gala Dinner & Show on Saturday night (valued at $329) is included with every Congress Pass. Additional guest tickets can be added during registration.

Is there a CPR session?

Yes. The CPR Workshop on Saturday afternoon is included for every delegate.

Are group discounts available?

Yes. Book 2 or more GPs together and save 52%, rising to 55% for 5+, 60% for 10+ and 65% for 15+ GPs. Group discounts end 30 December.

Are accommodation discounts available?

Yes. Discounted accommodation packages will be announced for each city.

When and where are future Congresses held?

In 2027: Melbourne (15–16 May), Sydney (26–27 June) and Brisbane (18–19 Sept). International Cruises and Dubai dates are to be confirmed.

For industry partners

What partnership packages are available?

Diamond, Platinum and Gold partnership packages, plus standard and non-profit exhibition booths. Pricing and inclusions are in the 2027 Partnership Prospectus, available on request.

How do partners meet delegates?

Every catering break is held in the Exhibition Hall: 7 breaks and about 6 hours of face-to-face time at each Congress.

Can we exhibit in more than one city?

Yes. Book two Congresses and save 8%, or all three and save 16% with Official National Partner status.

When will logistical details be provided?

An Exhibitor Manual is sent before each Congress, covering bump-in and bump-out times, venue access, freight and on-site requirements.

Are there compliance requirements?

Yes. All partner activity must comply with relevant industry regulations. Partners are responsible for making sure their materials meet applicable standards.

How do we secure a partnership?

A partnership is confirmed when we receive a signed agreement and payment. Space is limited and allocated first come, first served.
